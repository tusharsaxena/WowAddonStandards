# Ka0s WoW Addon Standard (v2.93.0, 2026-10-30)

**Status:** Source of truth. All audit deviation reports and `NEW_ADDON_CONTEXT.md` template content derive from this standard. When the standard changes, bump the date and version at the top of this file.

**Adherence:** Every Ka0s addon **MUST** be built to this standard and **MUST** reference it: <https://github.com/tusharsaxena/WowAddonStandards> (see `documentation`, `toc-file-§1`).

**Audience:** future Ka0s and any agent (human or LLM) authoring or maintaining a Ka0s addon.

**Substrate:** Ace3. The ecosystem and Ka0s collection are aligned on it; deviations from Ace3 are case-by-case and must be justified.

**License:** MIT. Hostile licenses (`All Rights Reserved`) are forbidden.

## Reading this document

Each section uses these markers:

- **MUST** — non-negotiable; deviations are bugs.
- **SHOULD** — strongly preferred; deviations require a comment in code explaining why.
- **MAY** — optional; pick when it fits.
- **MUST NOT** / **SHOULD NOT** — forbidden patterns with cited reasons.

**Cross-references use the `filename-§N` scheme.** Every section lives in its own file under
`standards/standards/` and numbers its subsections **locally, from 1**. Reference a whole section by
its **bare filename** (e.g. `architecture`, `audit-review-history`); reference a subsection as
**`<filename>-§<n>`** (e.g. `architecture-§5`, `options-ui-§10`). This is the **only** cross-reference
form — the old global `§N.M` numbering is retired. When you add or reorder a section, update the
[Sections](#sections) list here.

**Reference implementations are described, not named.** This normative standard **MUST NOT** name a specific addon (from the Ka0s collection or the wider ecosystem) as a reference implementation. Instead it *describes* the implementation — what it does and how — in enough detail to be actionable on its own. This keeps the standard self-contained and durable when addons change or are renamed. The **named** research evidence behind these patterns (which real addons exhibit them) lives in the companion research document, `INDUSTRY_RESEARCH.md`, which is a standards-process input rather than part of the normative standard.

Where a Ka0s addon today already implements a rule well, it is called out as *"reference implementation (in the collection)"* with a description of the addon's role, never its name.

`loadSuites` **raising** on a listed-but-absent suite rather than passing over it, which the kit does today, with a deliberate absence **declared** as a `pending` entry that registers as a skip carrying its reason; the conformance suites named at the point they are mandated — `tests/test_surface_parity.lua` for the stub-surface parity case, `tests/test_vendor_sync.lua` delegating to the kit rather than reimplementing it; the two vendored-payload gates — library-side kit-sync and consumer-side — and the skip as a third case status; characterization tests before a behavior-preserving refactor; and the green gate's **speed** as a governed property — measure before optimizing, never re-parse source already read, never spawn one process per item, and parallelism last and opt-in; and every out-of-game run **bounded** — per-process and process-tree memory, wall clock, re-launch depth, per-case heap and CPU budgets and a leak gate, all from the vendored kit, with no suite doing work at load or naming a host path.

- **[automated-tests](standards/automated-tests.md)** — the consolidated record of the four out-of-game suites: frozen `docs/automated-tests/<run>/` bundles, the vendored `tests/_kit/run-automated-tests.sh`, what gates (lint, tests) and what only records (perf, complexity), `RESULTS.md` as the single-path trend line — with **every row pinned to the commit SHA it measured and to whether the tree was clean at that SHA**, a dirty run marked as such, and a row predating the rule carried forward as *unknown* rather than clean, the two cells arriving with the runner that emits them at LibKa0s test-kit revision 25 (LibKa0s v1.55.0) — the `ANALYSIS.md` write-up, whose release run carries `## Preview and simulation data` (preview-mode, *Kept current at every release*), the **one boundary** between what is generated and what is authored — everything in `RESULTS.md` is the runner's output, the two commit cells included, and the complexity watch list's `Disposition` is the one authored cell in the file, pointing at layout-§1's census for an over-cap entry rather than ruling a second time — the **sighted complexity gate** — `lizard` measured through the kit's sanitized shadow (`#` and the Ruby-like reader's bare `it`, `class`, `module`, `begin`, `unless` are its documented Lua blind spots) with a function-count parity mismatch (`blindFiles` above 0) meaning complexity did not pass, from LibKa0s test-kit revision 35, and a **crashed** run — `lizard` raised or bounded — is complexity not passing too, its figures zeroed and its file named (`crashedFiles`, kit revision 41), and `bash tests/_kit/run-automated-tests.sh --suite complexity` the only command a gate line quotes — what a **complexity warning** means: CCN above 15 and nothing else, the runner passing `lizard` `-L 1500` so function length is layout-§1's alone — and the **release gate**: all four suites plus zero CCN > 15 and zero blind files at the tag, which the commit gate deliberately is not.

## Changelog

- **v2.93.0 (2026-10-30):** **Skin fonts and the owner's feedback: two new faces and a license rule, equal text widths, the font outline, the whole font block, the `skin` verb, HUD markers, hidden sections and a safe character set.** The owner's batch of 2026-10-30, given after the skin library follow-ups passed smoke (Ka0sAddonsCommonTasks `docs/2026-10-30-SKIN_FONTS_AND_FEEDBACK/`, `DECISIONS.md`), changes ten rules, all shipped by `LibKa0s v1.82.0`; each paragraph is one.

  **(1) library-stack-§8: which faces ship, and under what terms.** **The finding.** The owner asked for Ka0s Prototype, Ka0s Expressway and Ka0s Fira Sans Medium, and for every face no skin uses to leave the payload; two of the three are not OFL, the standard said "OFL" only in passing (skinning-§4), and §8 still listed five faces that left at Media minor 8. **The rule.** The catalog is the list and §8 no longer restates it. A face ships only under terms that allow redistributing it with a free addon (MUST): OFL by default, other terms only on a recorded owner ruling naming the face and its terms, with the terms file beside the face and named by the library's media test; Prototype ships on the owner's ruling (D-01), from its public distribution with its `Prototype.txt`, on its recorded terms ("free for personal use, freely distributed with Prototype.txt"). A face whose own terms forbid redistributing it never ships, whatever the ruling, and Expressway Regular was refused under that clause: the Typodermic Desktop EULA its author distributes it with puts embedding in software outside the free license (`LibKa0s` LK-01). A face no skin names leaves the payload with its terms file. Each face's locale mask is read from its shipped character map.

  **(2) skinning-§4: each skin's faces, and Ka0s Default's text.** **The rule.** Which face a skin draws is library data; from `v1.82.0` Ka0s Default draws Prototype, and WoW Forever Fira Sans Medium for body with SemiBold for titles and HUD (D-02); Obsidian keeps Inter and Inter SemiBold until the owner rules on its face again, because the Expressway D-02 named does not ship (open-evolutions). Ka0s Default's recorded exceptions gain its own face and bar texture (Oxanium from `v1.79.0`, Prototype from `v1.82.0`) and its default outline, so a pin of an unoutlined string or the client face moves.

  **(3) skinning-§4: text width is skin-invariant (MUST).** **The finding.** The same LootHistory window truncated text under some skins that it fit under Ka0s Default (owner item 5), and drag-strip titles were cut off in most skins (item 11). **The rule.** Every skin's body and title sizes are chosen so its text is about as wide as Ka0s Default's, measured on the shipped bytes with the outline on, and the library's suite pins the ratios (D-08). HUD text scales by a per-skin factor through `S.HudFontSize`, which never rewrites the stored size (D-09). A fixed-width label the library draws fits its text in every skin, re-measured on every repaint, and never truncates. High Contrast's "larger text" is gone from its line. WoW Default's sizes wait for a real Friz Quadrata measurement (open-evolutions).

  **(4) skinning-§4: an enabled control reads as enabled (MUST).** **The finding.** An accent button's label read as grayed out under three skins (owner item 16). **The rule.** A button's label keeps a live contrast against its fill in every shipped skin, and the library's suite guards it per skin and per button kind (D-25).

  **(5) skinning-§3: a skin change resets the overrides, and the tab's layout.** **The finding.** A font or texture override survived a change of skin (owner item 1). **The rule.** When the look the owner draws changes (`Skin:DrawsKey` before against after), the overrides return to the new skin's own in the same write, through the library's one chooser, `lib.__chooseSkin`: the tab, the first-run picker (preview, close and *Apply*) and its *Apply to all*. Never inside `Skin:SetChoice`; *Copy to every Ka0s addon…* and a raw `/<slash> set` reset nothing (D-10). **Reset font** and **Reset texture** sit under the dropdowns, each disabled while its half is the skin's own, and *Copy to every Ka0s addon…* moves under its own heading, *Other Ka0s addons* (D-11).

  **(6) skinning-§3 and §6: the *Font outline*.** **The finding.** The owner asked for outlined text by default, as a setting (item 13). **The rule.** A Skin-tab dropdown, stored at `skin.outline`: *All text* (the default), *HUD text only* or *Off*. The flag is the skin's `hud.outline` (`OUTLINE`); *All text* applies it to every string the library paints, *HUD text only* to HUD text, *Off* to none, and it never outlines the monospace role. *Ignore fonts* wins and grays the choice. *Copy to every Ka0s addon…* carries it, and a skin change does not reset it (D-06).

  **(7) options-ui-§16: every font setting is the whole block (MUST).** **The finding.** Some addons offered a face with no flags, or stored no flags at all. **The rule.** Every font setting carries at least face, size, color with its class-color companion, flags and shadow, composed by the library. In an adopted addon the *Font flags* row gains **From skin**, the default for every element, which resolves through `skin.outline` to the flag skinning-§3 resolves for `hud.outline` (none under *Off* or *Ignore fonts*); on the re-vendor a stored flag equal to the addon's previous default moves to *From skin* once, as a per-profile migration step, and only a flag the player picked wins (D-07, skinning-§6). An addon that has not adopted skins carries the rows with a plain flags default. Anti-pattern #107 gains a *From skin* flag resolved around the library's resolver and a skin-face HUD size that bypasses `S.HudFontSize`.

  **(8) slash-commands-§2 and skinning-§3: `/<slash> skin`.** **The rule.** `skin` is reserved, and registered in every addon that has adopted skins, directly after `config`, its act the library's `O.SkinVerb(spec)` (OptionsSkinPicker minor 3): the picker a new profile shows, whatever *picker seen* says. No argument, no alias; in combat a gray refusal on every call; a settings verb that is nonetheless refused while the addon is disabled, by ruling (MUST), and that a host never lists in its `liveVerbs`. Every Options stub owes `SkinVerb` from its re-vendor of `v1.82.0` (D-16).

  **(9) preview-mode: HUD displays may go unmarked, and no simulation marker floats over a HUD.** **The finding.** The owner asked to remove AuraMaster's per-container `TEST` placeholder (item 9) and KickCD's floating simulation strip (item 11); the first was required by v2.86.0's marker MUST, whose measured failure was a window. **The rule.** A HUD display (bars, icons, grids, containers and HUD text with no window chrome) **MAY** go unmarked in test mode; a window, whatever the addon calls it, keeps the MUST, and an unlocked drag strip's tag stays (D-14). The step panel is a running session's marker and stays on screen, built with `close = false` where no window strip marks the session; a HUD display **MUST NOT** carry a simulation marker of its own. The windows' simulation bands stay (D-13). Anti-pattern #97 is narrowed to match.

  **(10) options-ui-§22 and localization-§7, two new sections.** **options-ui-§22 (MUST).** Where a selector picks styles, modes or kinds, each with its own group of rows, the page shows the group of every value a selector picks and hides the rest, through the library's `shownWhen` (OptionsWidgets minor 22), whose `any` form (OR across selectors) ships in `v1.82.0`; a picked group stays shown, grayed, while its feature is off, and a host never hand-rolls it (owner item 10, D-17). **localization-§7 (MUST).** A string painted in a skinned face uses printable ASCII plus the set every shipped display face draws, and anything else becomes ASCII, a catalog mark or a mono-face glyph; game data, chat and test data are exempt, and the test kit's glyph guard fails a stray (owner item 5b, D-19). debug-logging-§2's stale sentence that every other surface uses stock Blizzard fonts is rescoped to an addon that has not adopted skins.

  **The ripple.** The index blurbs for library-stack, options-ui, preview-mode, skinning, slash-commands and localization; anti-patterns #97 and #107 (the range is unchanged at #1–#108); `AUDIT.md`'s *Check skinning* (d), (g) and the new (h) (the addon's own fixed widths), the marker and simulator checks, settings check (l) and the new *Check display characters* step (localization-§7); `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` (*One skin per addon*); `NEW_ADDON_CONTEXT.md` (hard rules 8a, 18 and 24b, the font-group lines, the #107 bullet and the definition-of-done lines); `NEW_ADDON.md` (the Options stub's members); open-evolutions closes the follow-ups run (merged 2026-10-30, `LibKa0s v1.81.0` tagged), records this run, its branch `feat/2026-10-30-skin-fonts-feedback` and its rulings, adds a line to the drag-handle question, and records the real Friz Quadrata measurement.
